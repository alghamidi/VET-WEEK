Background of Program

Context and Background

In Nigeria, the veterinary profession is at a crucial juncture, facing challenges and opportunities that mirror global trends. The country's growing population increases the demand for animal protein, placing a spotlight on livestock production, food safety, and the control of zoonotic diseases as such multi-sectoral collaboration is essential for achieving optimal health outcomes for animals, humans, and the environment. Nigeria is a hotspot for zoonotic diseases. Recent outbreaks, including highly pathogenic avian influenza, Lassa fever, and other emerging infectious diseases, have underscored the critical role of veterinarians as the first line of defence in disease surveillance, prevention, and control. The country's dense human-animal interface, driven by pastoralism, livestock markets, and the bushmeat trade, further elevates the risk of disease spill over, making the veterinary workforce indispensable for national security and public health.

The week-long event will be organized around key thematic pillars

and will include a series of activities to maximize participation, ensuring that " the name of the Association shall always resound" across the nation.

The objectives of AVMS ABU Zaria Vet Week 2026 are to:

  • Provide a comprehensive overview of current veterinary issues and innovations relevant to the Nigerian context, inspiring a new generation of "great learning".
  • Offer a platform for sharing student-led research, capstone projects, and innovative solutions.
  • Facilitate skills acquisition through practical workshops on topics like clinical techniques, laboratory diagnostics, and digital health tools.
  • Promote networking and dialogue among veterinary students, academics, practitioners, and policymakers.
  • Enhance public awareness on the vital role of veterinarians in safeguarding public health, ensuring food security, and promoting sustainable development.
  • Forge collaboration among students and professionals to create a supportive network for future leaders in veterinary medicine in Nigeria.

    • The expected outputs and Outcomes:

  • Policy briefs and advocacy documents to inform stakeholders on key veterinary and public health issues.
  • Media coverage and increased public awareness of the veterinary profession.
  • A strengthened network among veterinary students, academics, and professionals.
  • Enhanced knowledge and skills of veterinary students in key areas of veterinary medicine and public health.
  • Increased awareness among the public on the role of veterinarians in safeguarding human and animal health.
  • Strengthened collaboration between academia, government agencies, NGOs, and the private sector.
  • Promotion of entrepreneurship and innovation among veterinary students, creating pathways for self-reliance and job creation.
  • Improved veterinary practices and public health outcomes in the Zaria community.

    • AVMS ABU Zaria Vet Week 2026 is a vital platform for inspiring greatness among the next generation of veterinarians. By addressing critical issues such as pandemic preparedness, food safety, AMR, and entrepreneurship, the event contributes to the broader goals of public health, economic development, and national security. We invite all stakeholders’ academics, professionals, government agencies, NGOs, and the private sector to partner with us in making this event a resounding success.
