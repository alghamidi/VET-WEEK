About VET WEEK "26

Theme: Inspiring Greatness

The Association of Veterinary Medical Students (AVMS), Ahmadu Bello University (ABU), Zaria, is pleased to present this concept note for the annual Vet Week. This annual event is a significant platform for veterinary students to engage with critical issues impacting animal health, public health, Social and Cultural Heritage and the veterinary profession. By bringing together students, academics, and professionals, Vet Week serves as a cornerstone for professional development, community engagement, and advocacy, all while championing the enduring spirit of "Setting pace for the benefit of humanity ". The Vet Week aligns with the global demand for meaningful youth participation in advocacy and decision-making processes, recognizing that young people are not merely leaders of tomorrow but essential partners in shaping today’s policies and solutions. The empowers veterinary students to take ownership of pressing national and global challenges, from pandemic preparedness to food security, while equipping them with the skills and platforms to contribute actively to policy dialogues and governance structures. This event also aligns with University's legacy as a "citadel of great learning" and its motto of "Discipline, Self-Reliance, and Excellence". It will explore key thematic areas, including:

    1. The Role of Veterinary Medicine in Pandemic Preparedness and Response
    Community outreach and Public Health awareness campaigns
    3. Strengthening Food Safety and Security in Nigeria.
    4. Combating Antimicrobial Resistance (AMR) through Education and Stewardship
    5. Promoting Entrepreneurship and Innovation for Veterinary Graduates
    6. Intellectual Discourse
    7. Cultural Carnival
    8. Animal Rally
    9. Public lecture
    10. Dinner and Award Night

VET WEEK '26

Theme: Inspiring Greatness: Securing the Future of Animal Health, Public Health, and National Development

Featuring

Intellectual Discourse

A scientific conference featuring plenary sessions, panel discussions, and student research presentations on pressing veterinary and public health issues. This activity promotes academic excellence, critical thinking, and evidence-based solutions to challenges facing the veterinary profession in Nigeria.

FREE VETERINARY MEDICAL OUTREACH

Noiler: Sensitization

An outreach program targeting rural poultry farmers in communities surrounding Zaria. This activity involves educating farmers on the proper management, feeding, health care, and disease prevention strategies for Noiler birds, thereby improving rural livelihoods and food security.

Bon fire Night

An evening of relaxation, storytelling, and networking around a bonfire, featuring music from a DJ, an MC, and a vibrant atmosphere to foster camaraderie among students.

Excursions

Yankari Game Reserve, Kano Zoo and L & Z Integrated Farms, Clear Water, Emir Palace and Fifth Chucker

Dinner & Award Night

The grand finale of Vet Week, featuring a formal dinner, recognition and awards for outstanding students, entertainment, and speeches from distinguished guests. This event celebrates excellence and inspires greatness among the student body.

Agro Vet Student Investment Summit & Magazine Lunch

A high-profile lecture delivered by a distinguished veterinarian, academic, or public health expert. The lecture will address a topical issue in veterinary medicine, public health, or national development, inspiring students and the wider audience to think boldly about the future of the profession.
